11/14/26, 12:20PM - core/horizon (task 012, built by the horizon agent, reviewed by Boss): horizon(grid, &HorizonQuery) gives the skyline from an eye point. For each azimuth, walk out one grid cell at a time and keep the steepest slope (h - eye)/d; elev = atan(slope). No data in a direction = flat (elev 0, dist null). Flat earth on purpose (0.18 m drop at 1.5 km). ray_max_below adds an early exit (stop once even the grid's tallest building further out couldn't beat the best slope). It's only safe when the eye is below the grid top, which a review catch fixed and a test now covers. 14 tests. ~14 ms per full 360 deg on a 3 km grid.

11/14/26, 12:40PM - core/sunblock (task 013, built by the sunblock agent, reviewed by Boss): sun_block(skyline, lat, lon, date, utc_offset_s, event) says when buildings hide the sun and how many golden-hour minutes it stays in view. visible(t) is yes/no: blocked when skyline(az(t)) > max(sun_elev(t), 0). Walk a minute at a time from flat sunset back toward noon until it flips, then bisect to the second. Decision: golden minutes only count +6 deg down to flat sunset (below the horizon there's golden light but no sun to film). LA, June 21: open sky 36 min; a 10 deg wall WNW hides the sun at 19:10:11. Core takes a UTC offset; the server converts the IANA tz.

11/14/26, 1:25PM - core/search (task 014, built by the horizon agent, reviewed by Boss): search(grid, brief, utc_offset_s) ranks spots for clear / between / behind, on the ground or a roof. Candidates on a <=40x40 grid (also the heatmap). Roof = 3 m+ above the lowest surface within 50 m, and the spot must be flat enough to stand on. The sun path is computed once per search (moves <0.01 deg across 800 m). A fast pass scores everyone; the exact sun_block runs only for the picked spots. Scores are products of clamped linear ramps in score.rs (first-guess weights, to tune on real LA data). Top N at least 40 m apart. ~0.3 s for ~1000 candidates.

11/14/26, 3:50PM - core/tune (task 020, built by the horizon agent): first run of search on real downtown LA heights (Vexcel, 400 m around Pershing Square + 1500 m of skyline, cached in cache/). New example: `cargo run -p golden-core --release --example search_la -- [--ground|--roof] [--json]` prints the top spots for clear / between / behind on Jun 21 and Nov 14, with why lines and Google Maps links. ~0.5-0.8 s per search on the 3.8 km grid. Findings: roofs win everything; from the street the sun is behind buildings above 15 deg in June, so ground gets no spots at all, and in Nov only "behind" (sun hidden at 8-11 deg). One fix in score.rs: the fast pass counted a 20 s glimpse of the sun through a thin slot as "in view", which ranked a spot whose sun is really gone at 18 deg in the June behind top 5. Now the sun must be in view for 1 min in a row (MIN_IN_VIEW_S); +1 test. Weight changes are proposed to burvy, not made.
